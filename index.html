My Mission

Born and raised in the suburbs of Long Island New York, I was always surrounded by the air of urgency and diligence. My father was a first generation immigrant and I was able to experience what perseverance and hard work looked like first hand. I grew up in the same neighborhood my grandparents settled in when the first immigrated from Ireland. My parents always encouraged my creative side and when I was a child I would always succumb to my curiosity and indulge in different outlets. From painting to photography, or electronics to coding, I have always been just as excited to learn something new about something that infatuates me. I have stuck my head into every creative door that I’ve noticed and I always have a propensity for perfection. I like to create things that people will appreciate, wether that includes utility, functionality, beauty, or insightfulness, my goal is always to captivate and impress.

Education

After graduating from G.W. Hewlett High School in 2013 I was accepted into Savannah College of Art and Design in Georgia. I was interested in the arts and design but ended my experience when I couldnt decide on a career path. In 2016 I decided to start fresh and give myself the education I deserverd and achieved an Associets Degree in Liberal Arts from Nassau Community College. In the fall of 2019 I studied Real Estate at Molloy College and became certified as a Real Estate Agent. Since then I have taken a boot-camp for full stack developement and have learned many Web Development Skills.
